The 2026 Aotearoa New Zealand Physical Oceanography Workshop (**NZ-POW**) is underway **today and tomorrow**, 2–3 September 2026.

## Join in person or online

- **When:** Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 September 2026
- **Where:** [ESNZ Greta Point, Wellington](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZsvGQZ7VTw9A76zP6)
- **Online:** [Join on Microsoft Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com/meet/497152125432482?p=sSqVwibWGXMCVtnsLA)

[Download the programme (PDF)]({{ '/commons/POW2026.pdf' | relative_url }})
