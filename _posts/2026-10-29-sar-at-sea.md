## Abstract

How do we find people lost at sea, and those who do not want to be found at sea? One answer lies in the abilities offered by synthetic aperture radar (SAR) combined with deliberately making people more visible (radar reflectors) and using the surface of the sea itself as the sensing medium (wave spectral signatures). In this talk we introduce the research that has led to a new spin-out from UoA (Seascape Intelligence Ltd), and how a combination of new hardware with AI tooling have allowed us to get more from the complexities of SAR, in order to deliver insights into wave spectra and the human behaviours that disturb it.
