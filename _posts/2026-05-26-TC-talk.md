## Abstract

Climate change has led to increased heavy rain events in some part of the South Pacific where local and regional governments are looking for infrastructure solutions going forward. An Environmental Impact Assessment was conducted on the geology and forecast rainfall and fluvial runoff for Vava'u, Tonga to support the Ministry of Meteorology, Energy, Information, Disaster Management, Environment, Climate Change and Communications (MEIDECC) climate action plan. This presentation will present some of the findings of the EIA, with a focus on the estuarine lagoons of Vava'u.
