## New Zealand Physical Oceanography Workshop (POW) 2026

NZ-POW 2026 has been and gone. It was held 2–3 September 2026 at [ESNZ Greta Point, Wellington](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZsvGQZ7VTw9A76zP6), on the theme **The physics of the ocean in the age of multiple stressors**. It went well.
